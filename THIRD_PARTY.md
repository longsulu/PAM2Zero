# Source attribution

PAM2Zero uses the pair-code convention, processed off-target datasets and aligned D prior from CrisprPr:
https://github.com/mochew/CrisprPr

The compact competition and PAM2-zero model, component controls and analyses were organized from the experiment sources listed in `docs/source_manifest.json`. The D initialization and trainable lookup principle are inherited from CrisprPr.

The activity extension follows the archived CrisprFusion source/data protocol:
https://github.com/cwk644/CrisprFusion
Commit: `d696e35df4291aa5af9993845b4dee45a0132bfd`.
Its verification manifest is stored as package data. Original data and source files retain their upstream terms.

TrueOT source:
https://github.com/baolab-rice/CRISPR_OT_scoring

MOFF measurements, Chaudhari replicate/capture data and thermodynamic tables remain external inputs and should be cited to their original publications. They are not redistributed here.
